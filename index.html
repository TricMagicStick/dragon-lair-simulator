DRAGON LAIR

You are an ancient dragon. Your mountain cave holds the hoard. Raid the valley towns, steal their sheep, recover lost eggs, and return them to your lair.

Mouse — Look / pitch & yaw (click to lock)
W / Space — Flap wings (thrust + lift)
S — Brake / slow
A / D — Bank left / right
E — Pick up sheep or egg (when close)
G — Drop cargo (near cave = add to hoard)
F — Breathe fire (raid a town)
Esc — Unlock mouse / pause
